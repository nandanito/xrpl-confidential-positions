# REVIEW-061 leg B — a newcomer looks at the diagrams

You are looking at a small public website for the first time. You arrived from a
LinkedIn post. You know roughly what a blockchain is and that the XRP Ledger is
one, and you may work in finance, product or software. You have never read
XLS-96 or this project's repository, and you will not.

**Read only the screenshots listed below, in this order, with the Read tool. Do
not open any other file, do not search, do not run commands, and do not look
anything up.** Everything you may use is in these images.

The site is a read-only demonstration from a research project. It has just
added diagrams to its pages, to show its architecture, its flows, and which
information is public and which is hidden. The project's first priority is
that every statement is true and claims no more than the evidence; your job is
the second: whether a stranger understands the diagrams, and whether any of
them leads a stranger to believe something the page does not say.

Answer in this order.

1. **From the front page's diagrams alone**, in three or four sentences of your
   own: what is hidden, what is public, who can read what, and what this site is.
2. **From the explainer's diagrams**, briefly and in your own words: how tokens
   go in, move and come out; who can read what; what the facility example shows;
   how the project runs on someone's machine. Where your account is wrong or
   vague, the diagram failed you there, so be honest about it.
3. **The other pages' diagrams**: what each tells you, in a sentence.
4. **Where a diagram confused or misled you**: an arrow you read the wrong way,
   a colour you read as meaning something else, a box whose label you did not
   understand, a diagram that contradicts the text near it, or one too dense to
   follow. Name the page and quote the label exactly.
5. **On the phone screenshots**: anything that becomes hard to follow.
6. **What is missing or could be cut**.

For each point in 4 to 6, give the smallest change that would fix it. No praise
is needed; the project uses only the problems. End with the line
`END OF LEG B REPORT`.
