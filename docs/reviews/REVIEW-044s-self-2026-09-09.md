## S3 — the printed disclosure claims more coverage than the detector has

Disclosure 4 says rule 5 counts `**` only, and names CAPITALS, single-`*`
italics and sentence length as what escapes it. It does **not** name `__bold__`,
which CommonMark renders as `<strong>`, or `<b>`/`<strong>` themselves.
Disclosure 3 says a `⚠` outside backticks is counted, and does not say that
`⚠️` (U+26A0 + VS-16) and `&#9888;` are not counted at all.

**Grade: MEDIUM**, and it is the disclosure rather than the detector: a lexical
gate over a named set is allowed to miss things, but not to name the wrong set.
