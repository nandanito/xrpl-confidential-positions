### 2. MEDIUM — new defect. "Neither got the composition whole" overstates leg A, and it was copied into the plan

`REVIEW-048c` finding 10, the plan's F-13 bullet and the handover all say both external legs re-derived 1276 B and 1210 B *"and neither got the composition whole"*.

- Leg B wrote *"1177 + 33 = 1210"*. **Wrong** — that uses the n=4 base for the n=3 case.
- Leg A wrote *"adds 99 bytes for n=4 and 66 for n=3, giving 1276 and 1210 exactly"*. **Right**, if each delta is taken from the paper's total at the same n, which is the natural reading. It is terse and not wrong.

So one leg of two got it wrong, not both. The finding stands, because a reader of the row alone got the composition wrong, but the evidence offered for it is overstated at three sites. **Fix the plan and the handover. The adjudication is a historical record and is corrected in `REVIEW-049c`, not edited.**
